**1. About the BYOS Program**

1.1. The BYOS program (the "Program") allows a user ("you" or the "Suffix Owner") to connect a registered second-level domain under a supported top-level domain ("Your Domain") to the technical infrastructure of the PUBLIC Free Suffix Service Project ("we", "our", "us", or the "Service"), so that third-level sub-domains under Your Domain (the "Suffix") can be registered by eligible users through the Service.

1.2. **Important Disclaimer on the Nature of the Program.** We provide technical DNS hosting and a sub-domain registration program for Your Domain. **We are not a registrar or a registry for Your Domain.** Your domain registration relationship remains solely between you and your own domain registrar, including all renewal obligations, registrar fees, and registrar policies. Nothing in the BYOS program transfers, assumes, or replaces any part of that relationship.

1.3. **Retention of Ownership and Revocability.** You retain ownership of Your Domain as governed by your registrar and the applicable registry. However, **the operation of Your Domain as a registrable suffix under the Program is a revocable service privilege granted by us, not a property right, and it may be suspended, restricted, or terminated by us at any time for any reason in accordance with this Onboarding Agreement.**

1.4. Submitting an onboarding application incurs a one-time application fee in community points, at the amount published in the onboarding interface (currently 2,500 points). **The fee is automatically refunded to your account if the application is not approved**, including rejection in review, expiry of the verification window, or withdrawal before approval; the fee is consumed once the application is approved. Any community points (TGC) involved in related services are community points of the Nssnap Community platform, are not money, and are non-refundable except where this Onboarding Agreement explicitly provides for a refund.

**2. Eligibility Requirements**

2.1. **Identity Verification.** You must complete the identity verification level published for the BYOS program in the onboarding interface (currently Level 2 verification or above). Applications from accounts that do not meet the published verification level will be rejected.

2.2. **Domain Format.** Your Domain must be a second-level domain consisting of exactly two labels under a top-level domain published in the IANA root zone database. Second-level suffixed domains (such as domains under co.uk or other composite public suffixes), internationalized domain names (IDN) at either the top-level or the second level, and labels exceeding the technical limit of 63 bytes per label are not supported. The domain must be a name that you are genuinely entitled to manage.

2.3. **Domain Validity.** Your Domain must have a remaining validity of not less than two years at the time it enters review, as further described in Section 7.

2.4. **Uniqueness and Conflicts.** One domain may exist in the BYOS program only once. If Your Domain is already being onboarded or has been onboarded by another account, or already exists as a suffix of the Service, your application will be refused. If you believe you have a legitimate claim to a domain that has been onboarded by another account, please contact us through the Meta Board of the Nssnap Community forum.

**3. Application, Delegation and Review**

3.1. You submit your application through the onboarding interface and select a visibility mode for the Suffix. In Shared mode, any eligible user may see the Suffix and register sub-domains under it. In Private mode, only you can see and register sub-domains under it. You may change this mode later subject to the interface rules.

3.2. **Delegation Within the Verification Window.** After your application is submitted, you must point the nameservers of Your Domain, at your registrar, to the designated nameservers published in the onboarding interface, within the verification window shown to you (currently 72 hours). **If the delegation is not completed within the window, your application will automatically expire and the reserved hosting zone will be released, without liability on our side.** You may submit a new application at any time afterwards.

3.3. **Manual Review.** Applications that pass delegation verification and the validity check enter a manual review queue. We may approve or reject an application at our reasonable discretion, and a rejection will be accompanied by a stated reason. If your application is rejected, you are welcome to share feedback or seek assistance through the Meta Board, or submit a new application after addressing the stated reason. **A rejection decision is final for that application.**

3.4. **Control Warranty.** By submitting an application, you represent and warrant that you control Your Domain, including the ability to modify its nameserver delegation, and that your application complies with all the terms of this Onboarding Agreement.

**4. Your Suffix Controls**

4.1. As the Suffix Owner, you may configure the Suffix through the Service interface, within the limits published there, including the visibility mode (Shared or Private), the minimum and maximum registrable label length for the Suffix (within the technical limit of 63 bytes per label), a per-user registration quota or an unlimited quota, and the switch to enable or pause new registrations for the Suffix.

4.2. **Platform Priority.** We may disable registration for any Suffix for abuse prevention, legal compliance, or operational reasons. **A platform-side disable takes priority over your settings, and it cannot be lifted from your side.**

**5. Your Responsibilities as a Suffix Owner**

5.1. **First-Line Responsibility.** You are the first line of responsibility for the operation of the Suffix and for the use of all sub-domains registered under it. You must respond to abuse reports relating to the Suffix, take reasonable and timely action against sub-domains that violate the AUP or this Onboarding Agreement, and cooperate with our investigations in good faith and within a reasonable time.

5.2. **Domain Maintenance.** You must maintain Your Domain in good standing with your registrar, keep its registration information true, accurate, and complete, and keep its remaining validity not less than two years by renewing it with your registrar in time. **Consequences of losing Your Domain, including the loss of all sub-domains registered under the Suffix, are borne by you.**

5.3. **No Impersonation.** The Suffix must not be used, and its naming must not be reasonably likely, to impersonate or confuse with the names, trademarks, or services of other persons, organizations, or top-level domains.

5.4. **Assistance in Governance.** You must promptly report to us any illegal or violating activity conducted through sub-domains under the Suffix, and use the Suffix lawfully and compliantly yourself.

5.5. **No Circumvention.** You must not repoint Your Domain away from the designated nameservers while the Suffix is active, transfer the Suffix operation to another party, or otherwise use the BYOS program to circumvent any rules of the Service.

**6. Domain Validity Monitoring and Hold State**

6.1. We periodically monitor the registry expiry of Your Domain through public registry data services as published. When the remaining validity falls below two years, we will notify you to renew. **If the remaining validity stays below two years for the consecutive period published in the interface, the Suffix will enter a hold state in which new registrations and renewals under the Suffix are paused. DNS resolution of existing records is not affected by the hold state itself.**

6.2. **Consequences of Domain Loss.** If Your Domain expires at your registrar and is lost, delegated away by you, or otherwise ceases to be under your control, we may suspend or terminate the Suffix operation immediately, and the fate of sub-domains registered under the Suffix follows Your Domain. We are not liable for any such consequences.

**7. Service Records on the Zone**

7.1. You agree that we may set or maintain A and AAAA records at the zone apex and at the nic, www, report, and abuse hostnames of Your Domain, pointing to pages operated for feedback and contact purposes. You may manage these records through the zone panel, and you are encouraged to keep them intact so that third parties can reach the relevant contact pages. Our own abuse-reporting channels remain available regardless of these records.

**8. DNSSEC**

8.1. DNSSEC is not supported for suffixes in the BYOS program at present, and no related options are provided in the interfaces. You acknowledge this limitation and accept that no DNSSEC signing obligation applies to us for Your Domain.

9.3. **Effects of Termination.** Upon termination of the Suffix operation, sub-domains registered under the Suffix may stop functioning, and their registration outcomes follow the situation of Your Domain. No compensation of any kind is provided.
