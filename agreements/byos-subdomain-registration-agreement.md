**1. About Sub-domain Registration Under BYOS Suffixes**

1.1. A BYOS suffix (the "Suffix") is a registered second-level domain that has been onboarded to the Service by a third-party user (the "Suffix Owner") through the Bring Your Own SLD program (the "Program"). **A BYOS suffix is different from the official suffixes operated directly by the PUBLIC Free Suffix Service Project.** Its naming space originates from a domain owned and maintained by the Suffix Owner, not by the project.

1.2. Visibility modes of a Suffix are set by the Suffix Owner. In Shared mode, any eligible user may see the Suffix and register sub-domains under it. In Private mode, only the Suffix Owner can see and register sub-domains under it, and the Suffix is not listed publicly.

1.3. Where this Sub-domain Agreement and any published rule of the Suffix Owner conflict, the stricter provision applies to the extent it does not conflict with the rules of the Service. The rules of the Service always take priority.
